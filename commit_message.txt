1.14.441: lfs-kernel knows the ThinkPad P14s Gen 6 AMD; firmware built into the image

- lfs-kernel: hardware rows for Ryzen AI 300 laptops -- amdgpu-strix,
  amd-npu, mt7925, bluetooth-mtk, amd-audio, amd-mic, amd-laptop -- and a
  machine row, thinkpad-p14s-gen6-amd, that enables them all (@name rows)
- GPU, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, speaker/HDMI audio and the platform drivers are
  BUILT IN; the camera (UVC) and the internal microphones (AMD ACP) are
  modules, so they can be switched by loading/unloading
- lfs-kernel/kernel.conf: FIRMWARE_BUILTIN -- patterns matched against the
  installed firmware become CONFIG_EXTRA_FIRMWARE (no initramfs: a built-in
  driver needs its blobs inside the image); a row's fifth field fills it;
  a built-in amdgpu with no amdgpu blob stops the build
- the MT7925 and Ryzen AI NPU firmware directories are added to
  FIRMWARE_ONLY by the rows; README: one line for the machine profile
